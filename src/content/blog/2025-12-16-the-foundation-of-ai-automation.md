AI demands are bringing major problems in the business world today.

On one side, you have the Real Business. This is how your company actually works. It is the supply chain, the factories, and the sequence of tasks from your workforce.

On the other side, you have the **AI Dream**. Everyone wants to use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to automate tasks and make decisions faster. Most companies try to jump straight from the "Real Business" to the "AI Dream." They put modern AI tools on top of old, messy data systems.

The result is not intelligence, but failure. The AI gives wrong answers (hallucinations) because it is reading from different sources instead of **one source of truth**.

Tovy is founded to solve this specific problem. We believe you cannot automate a process if you have not engineered the process' data first.

> The term "Agent" can be defined in several ways. Anthropic makes an important architectural distinction between **Workflows**, where LLMs follow predefined code, and **Agents**, where LLMs dynamically direct their own processes and tool usage to accomplish tasks. 

Source: [building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)

An AI Agent is only as smart as the information you give it. In a university, giving AI good information is easy. In a real company, it is very hard.

Company knowledge is messy. It is hidden in PDF contracts, long email chains, and millions of rows of tables. To an AI, a raw ERP table looks like noise, not knowledge.

> We see lacking Reliability as an effect of missing data engineering.

We need to turn this messy business data into structured "Context" that an AI can understand. This is modern Knowledge Management. It is not about writing documents; it is about **engineering facts**.

Tovy’s approach comes from experience in Supply Chain Management and Process Optimization.

In a supply chain, data quality is critical. If the data about a product's weight is wrong, the truck will be overweight, and the shipment will stop. In the physical world, bad data costs real money immediately. We understand that Business Process Data is the most important asset you have. If you do not understand how a "Purchase Requisition" becomes a "Goods Receipt," you cannot build a data pipeline that works.

To connect systems with AI, Tovy builds a robust "Data Supply Chain". We start by extracting data securely, utilizing loading strategies that pull only necessary information to protect your system's performance. Next, we perform transformations to clean, fix duplicates, and achieve clear business logic. Finally, we implement automated Quality Assurance gatekeepers that detect and stop bad data before it reaches your AI, ensuring your automation is built on a foundation of facts.

*   **The Single Truth**: We create "Gold" tables. These tables are the undeniable truth of the business.
*   **Vector Search**: For text documents (like contracts or logs), we convert the text into a format the AI can search by meaning, not just by keywords.
*   **The Knowledge Graph**: We map connections between things. For example, "How is this Supplier related to this Raw Material?" This helps the AI understand relationships.

## The Result: Automation You Can Trust
When a client asks, "Can we build an AI Agent to check our inventory?" Tovy answers: "Yes, but first we must build the context."

An AI Agent built on Tovy’s infrastructure does not guess. When it needs to know where a shipment is, it looks at the Engineered Context we built. It finds facts, not probabilities.

This approach offers three key benefits:
*   **Less Hallucination**: The AI makes fewer mistakes because the data is clean.
*   **More Trust**: You know exactly where the AI got its answer.
*   **Scalability**: Once the foundation is ready, you can build many different AI tools on top of it.
