The landscape of data engineering is shifting. We are moving away from manual ETL pipelines toward self-healing, AI-integrated data fabrics.

### What is a Unified Data Fabric?

A data fabric is an architecture that facilitates the end-to-end integration of various data pipelines and cloud environments by using intelligent and automated systems. At Tovy, we specialize in building these ecosystems so that your team doesn't have to spend hours on manual maintenance.

### Why Automation Matters Now

As business growth accelerates, data bottlenecks become fatal. If your engineers are spending 80% of their time fixing broken connectors, they aren't building value. Automation isn't just a "nice to have"—it's the core requirement for scaling in 2026.

In this resource, we explore the tools and strategies that make "hands-off" data management a reality.
