Privacy Statement

Privacy is important to Tovy. We respect your privacy regarding information collected via this website, in full compliance with GDPR.

// 01. PROCESSING_CONTROLLER

Tovy is the controller for personal data collected through this website. For questions, contact us via: info@tovy.eu

// 02. DATA_COLLECTION_AND_USE

We only ask for data necessary to provide our services. We collect it lawfully with your explicit consent, based on contractual necessity or legitimate interest.

Project request form: We collect contact and project details. This is stored in Google Cloud Firestore to assess your request.
Server logs: Firebase Hosting records requests (IP, browser, pages visited) to maintain security.
Cookies: We use necessary cookies to manage consent preferences. We do not use tracking cookies without your explicit prior consent.

// 03. DATA_LOCATION_AND_TRANSFER

To protect your data, our primary database (Cloud Firestore) is hosted within the EU (region 'eur3'). Any international transfers by subcontractors are done via legally permitted mechanisms, such as standard contractual clauses.

// 04. RETENTION_AND_SECURITY

We retain data only as long as necessary for our services or as required by law. We secure your data with current industry measures to prevent loss, theft, or unauthorized access.

// 05. RIGHTS_UNDER_THE_GDPR

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your data:

Right to information: Know how your data is collected/used
Right of access: Request a copy of your information
Right to rectification: Correct incorrect data
Right to erasure: Request data deletion
Right to restriction: Temporarily pause processing
Right to portability: Receive data in a machine-readable format
Right to object: Object to the processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us via the e-mail address below.
